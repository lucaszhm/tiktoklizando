# Terms of Service

**ViajanteUp**
Last updated: 10 October 2026

## 1. What this application is

ViajanteUp is a personal desktop application that schedules and publishes
its owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account. It runs locally on
the owner's computer. It is not a hosted service, it is not sold, and it is not
made available to other users.

## 2. Who may use it

Only the owner of the application and of the connected TikTok account. There
are no accounts to create, no subscriptions and no payments.

## 3. What it does

* Uploads video files from the owner's computer to TikTok through TikTok's
  official Content Posting API.
* Lets the owner write a caption, choose who can watch the video, and choose
  whether comments, Duet and Stitch are allowed, before anything is published.
* Keeps a local list of which videos are scheduled for which date.

Every publication is started deliberately by the owner, who reviews the caption
and the settings for that specific video before it goes out.
